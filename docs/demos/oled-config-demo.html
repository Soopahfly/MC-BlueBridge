Adapter controls

Display and physical controls are independent. None disables physical menu controls. OLED off blanks the display and stops screen updates. Changes are saved on the adapter and applied after restart.

Mouse and aiming

Connect an adapter to check supported touchpad features.

Steam Controller gyro aiming (experimental)

Requires mouse output enabled above and single-controller MiSTer mode. Right-pad movement still works for fine adjustment. The pause button is reserved for aiming; trigger-to-fire replaces the gamepad right trigger. These global settings apply after restart. Relative aiming cannot automatically centre the crosshair. Mouse support depends on the MiSTer core. Steam Controller only; DS4 gyro is not included.
