DS4 mouse controls

Configure optional right-click and two-finger vertical scrolling. Implemented; hardware testing is pending.

Mouse buttons and scrolling

Requires single-controller MiSTer mode. These settings preserve your gyro and lightbar choices.

A selected controller button is reserved for right-click while mouse output is active. Avoid sharing it with your gyro pause button. The click type stays held until you release, even if your fingers move.

Lower values scroll faster. Suggested: 32 fast, 64 normal, 128 slow. The pointer stays still during a two-finger scroll, including gyro motion. Lifting a finger returns to pointer movement without a jump.

Open in desktop Chrome or Edge with the adapter connected by USB. Close other apps using its serial port.
