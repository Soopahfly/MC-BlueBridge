BlueBridge OLED

Choose your physical menu control and configure the optional fire-button animation.

Adapter controls

Display and physical controls are independent. None disables physical menu controls. OLED off blanks the display and stops screen updates. Changes are saved on the adapter and applied after restart.

Connect an adapter to read its settings.

PEW PEW animation

This only changes the OLED animation. It does not remap your controller or activate the profile you are editing.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer with the adapter connected by USB. Close other apps using its serial port.
