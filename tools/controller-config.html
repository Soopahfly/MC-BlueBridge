Mouse and aiming

Connect an adapter to check supported touchpad features.

Steam Controller gyro aiming (experimental)

Requires mouse output enabled above and single-controller MiSTer mode. Right-pad movement still works for fine adjustment. The pause button is reserved for aiming; trigger-to-fire replaces the gamepad right trigger. These global settings apply after restart. Relative aiming cannot automatically centre the crosshair. Mouse support depends on the MiSTer core. These controls apply to the Steam Controller; DS4 has separate controls below.

DualShock 4 gyro aiming (experimental)

Requires master mouse output above and single-controller MiSTer mode. Factory calibration must be received and validated before gyro aiming becomes active. R3 is the default pause button. Touchpad movement remains available for fine adjustment. Pause is reserved for aiming; optional R2 fire replaces gamepad R2. Relative aiming cannot automatically centre the crosshair. Accelerometer readings are diagnostic only; tilt mapping is not included.
